Understanding the EUR/USD Pair

The EUR/USD pair represents the exchange rate between the Eurozone and the United States. Buying EUR/USD means purchasing Euros while selling US Dollars, and selling it means selling Euros and buying Dollars.

This pair acts as a benchmark in Forex trading because of its high liquidity and generally narrow spreads. With Signals2Trade’s insights, traders can interpret these fluctuations accurately and make strategic trading decisions.
